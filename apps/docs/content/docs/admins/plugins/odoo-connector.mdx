---
title: Odoo connector
description: Import customers, their people and the product catalog with price lists from an Odoo ERP and keep them current.
---

The **Odoo connector** plugin (`odoo-connector`) reads customers from an Odoo ERP and keeps them in the CRM as accounts, with their people as contacts. It also brings in Odoo's product catalog and chosen price lists, so the CRM prices exactly like Odoo. It reads from Odoo, and writes there only when you turn on sending orders: then READY orders become draft sale orders in Odoo (see [Orders](#orders)).

## What it does

- Every Odoo customer (a company or a person with a customer rank above zero, no parent, active) becomes an account. An existing account is reused when it matches (see below); otherwise a new one is created.
- People under those customers (child contacts of type "contact") become contacts on the account.
- After the first import, the plugin checks Odoo for changed partners every `syncMinutes` minutes and updates the CRM.

## Before you start

- Odoo 18 or later (the plugin uses Odoo's JSON-2 API).
- An Odoo user for the CRM with an API key. It needs read access to contacts (partners), countries, users, products, product categories, taxes, price lists, sales orders, deliveries and invoices. It needs write access only for sending orders: create on contacts and sales orders.

## Install and settings

Install it under **Admin → Plugins → Odoo connector**.

| Setting | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `url` | none | The Odoo address, starting with `https://`, for example `https://odoo.example.com`. |
| `database` | none | The Odoo database name. |
| `syncMinutes` | `15` | Minutes between syncs, 5 to 1440. The check runs every 5 minutes, so a sync can start up to 5 minutes later. |
| `defaultCountry` | `CZ` | Two-letter country code for customers without a country in Odoo. |
| `dryRun` | on | While on, the sync only writes to the plugin log what it would create or change, and the scheduled sync is paused: use **Sync now**. |
| `priceLists` | empty | The Odoo price lists to import: their Odoo ids, separated by commas, for example `242, 245`. See [Catalog](#catalog). |
| `sendOrders` | off | Send orders in READY to Odoo as draft sale orders. While off, the connector never writes to Odoo. |
| `fillOwners` | on | Give an existing account without an owner the CRM user of its Odoo salesperson. See [Owners](#owners). |
| `timeZone` | `UTC` | The time zone the plugin's screens and emails show times in, for example `Europe/Prague`. |

The secret `apiKey` holds the Odoo user's API key. It is stored encrypted.

## Dry run first

A new install starts in dry run. Use **Test connection** to check the address, database and key: it shows the Odoo version and the API user's name. Then click **Sync now** and read the plugin log: it lists every account and contact the sync would create or update, and every customer that matches more than one account. When the list looks right, turn `dryRun` off in the settings and click **Sync now** again.

## How customers are matched

For each Odoo customer the plugin tries, in this order:

1. an account it already linked to this customer;
2. an account with the same country and registration number (spaces are ignored; Czech numbers are compared with leading zeros to 8 digits);
3. an account with the same VAT number;
4. otherwise it creates a new account.

When two or more accounts match in step 2 or 3, or the matching account is already linked to another Odoo customer, the plugin links nothing and lists the customer under **Customers matching more than one account** on the plugin's admin page. Fix it in the CRM (correct or remove the duplicate) and the next sync links it, even if nothing changed in Odoo.

## Owners

An account gets an owner when the customer's salesperson in Odoo has the same email (upper or lower case does not matter) as an active CRM user. The plugin takes the Odoo user's email, or the login when the email is empty. Otherwise the account has no owner and appears on the **Needs owner** page (under Plugins in the menu, for managers and admins), with the Odoo salesperson's name.

New accounts get the owner when the sync creates them. With `fillOwners` on, an existing account without an owner gets one the next time its customer changes in Odoo, for example when you create the salesperson's CRM user and then reassign or edit the customer in Odoo. A sync never replaces an owner that is already set. The new owner starts the account's protection like any other assignment.

## What Odoo controls

Odoo wins for these account fields: name, registration number, VAT number, billing street, city, postal code, region, country, email and phone. When a customer changes in Odoo, the next sync writes these fields to the account, even if someone edited them in the CRM. A field that is empty in Odoo never clears a value filled in the CRM.

The sync never touches anything else: an owner already set in the CRM, the shipping address, the price list, notes, activities and all other fields. The account's **Odoo** panel shows the link to the Odoo partner and when it was last synced.

## Archived customers

When a customer is archived in Odoo, or stops being a customer, nothing is deleted in the CRM. The account's Odoo panel shows the change. If the customer becomes active again, it syncs as before.

## Catalog

Every sync also brings in the catalog:

- **Categories:** all Odoo product categories, as a tree.
- **Products:** every sellable Odoo product variant becomes a product, with its SKU, price, cost, sales tax, unit and category. A variant that is archived or no longer sold in Odoo is archived in the CRM, never deleted.
- **Price lists:** the lists in `priceLists`, with all their rules. Lists they build on (for example a base list that is archived in Odoo) come along automatically as inactive lists, marked **Base for other lists**; reps don't pick them, but prices are computed through them.
- **Account price lists:** a linked account gets its Odoo customer's price list, when that list is imported. The account's Odoo panel shows the Odoo list, or that it is not imported.

Products, categories and these price lists are read-only in the CRM: change them in Odoo. To choose lists, click **Load price lists** on the plugin's admin page: it shows every Odoo list with its id. Put the ids in `priceLists` and save.

A list you remove from `priceLists` stays in the CRM as an inactive list, so older orders keep it, and is no longer given to accounts. A list is skipped, with a line in the plugin log, when a list it builds on can't be read with the Odoo user's access, or when it builds on more than 10 other lists.

Rules the CRM cannot use are listed on the admin page under **Rules the CRM cannot use**. The same list notes rules Odoo ranks differently from the CRM (a rule on one product variant next to a rule on the whole product). A product created by hand in the CRM with the same SKU as an Odoo variant is taken over: it keeps its id (so orders and price list rules that use it stay intact), becomes the Odoo product and is kept current from Odoo from then on; the plugin log says "Took over CRM product …". A deleted CRM product, or a product of another plugin, with the same SKU is never taken over: that variant is not imported, the plugin log names it and the next syncs try again.

## Compare with Odoo

**Compare with Odoo** checks that the CRM prices like Odoo. For each list in `priceLists` it picks up to 20 products, covering the kinds of rules on the list, and prices each at quantity 1, at the minimum quantity of the rule that prices it, and one below. Odoo prices the same lines without saving anything. The admin page then shows, per list, how many prices matched, and every difference with the reason:

- **price rule:** the CRM and Odoo apply a rule differently. Report it.
- **exchange rate:** Odoo converted the product price into the list currency with its rate of the day, and the CRM's rate for that day differs. Keep the CRM's exchange rates in line with Odoo's.

## Failures

**Sync now** and **Compare with Odoo** are queued and start within 5 minutes. A failing sync is logged in the plugin log with the reason, for example when Odoo rejects the API key, or denies access to a model the Odoo user cannot read (the log names it). The next run repeats everything since the last good run; a customer or person that failed on its own is tried again on every run until it succeeds. After three failed runs in a row, admins get one email.

## Orders

With `sendOrders` on (and dry run off), an order that becomes **READY** goes to Odoo within seconds; a check every 5 minutes picks up anything missed.

What the connector does in Odoo, and nothing else:
- finds the customer (by the account's link, registration number, then VAT; by exact name when the account has neither) or **creates** it and links the account;
- uses the customer or one of its delivery addresses when the order's delivery address matches, or **creates** a delivery address;
- **creates** a draft sale order: the CRM order number in *Source document* (`origin`), the CRM unit prices, the account's Odoo price list (or the customer's), the salesperson by email, the rep's name and the order note in the note.

Odoo confirms the order; the connector never confirms, edits or cancels it.

The Odoo user needs **create** on contacts (`res.partner`) and sales orders (`sale.order`, `sale.order.line`) and **read** on everything else the connector reads. No delete, no accounting rights.

### Status

| Odoo | Order status |
|---|---|
| quotation | SENT |
| confirmed | CONFIRMED |
| everything shipped | DELIVERED |
| fully invoiced | INVOICED |
| fully invoiced and every invoice paid | PAID |
| cancelled | CANCELLED |

Statuses are read on every sync (`syncMinutes`). They only move forward; partly shipped or partly paid orders keep the earlier status and the **Odoo** panel on the order shows the details: deliveries, invoices with what is left to pay, a "Price differs from Odoo" list when Odoo's price list would charge more than 1 more or less than the order, and a note when someone changed the order's total in Odoo.

When the connector can no longer find the Odoo order, the order keeps its status and the panel says "Not found in Odoo"; admins get one email. Odoo gives the same answer for a deleted order and for one the API user lost access to, so the connector never cancels on its own: if the Odoo order was deleted, cancel the CRM order by hand.

### When sending fails

- **Odoo unreachable:** the order stays READY and goes out when Odoo is back; the panel shows since when it waits. An order reopened or cancelled while it waits starts afresh when it is submitted again.
- **Odoo refuses access** (wrong key or missing rights): sending pauses for every order and admins get one notification. Fix the rights, then click **Test connection**.
- **Odoo refuses the order:** after 5 refusals, or at once for a product that does not come from Odoo, a currency that differs from the Odoo price list, or an order already in Odoo with other lines, the order becomes **SYNC_FAILED** and admins are notified. Fix the cause and click **Retry** on the order.

Sending is safe to repeat: whatever fails in between, each order exists once in Odoo.

## Orders from Odoo

Every sync also brings in the **confirmed** Odoo orders of customers linked to an account, including everything from before the link. Quotations and orders cancelled before they were imported stay out. An imported order:

- is read-only in the CRM, numbered from the CRM's order series, owned by the account's owner, with the Odoo order date, currency and note;
- has its product lines at Odoo's unit price, after any line discount. Notes, section headings and down payments are skipped. A product that is archived in Odoo (for example an old batch) is added to the CRM as an archived product, so the order is complete; reps cannot order it. Only a line whose product cannot be created in the CRM is left out, and the order note says how many lines are not shown; once the product exists, the next sync fills those lines in. VAT comes from the CRM product, so the CRM total can differ slightly; the order's **Odoo** panel then shows Odoo's total;
- follows Odoo's status like an order sent from the CRM, and its lines are updated when they change in Odoo.

Orders the CRM sent to Odoo are never imported again. If an imported order moves to another customer in Odoo, the CRM order keeps its account, its panel says "Customer changed in Odoo" and admins get one email. At most 200 orders are imported per sync; a large first import spreads over several syncs. An order that cannot be imported (for example its currency does not exist in the CRM) is listed on the admin page and tried again on every sync.

### The Odoo tab on the account

Linked accounts get an **Odoo** tab: the account's orders that exist in Odoo (newest 50, with the Odoo number, total without VAT and status), the open invoices and credit notes with what is owed (overdue ones in red), and the confirmed orders without VAT for this year and last year. The tab shows the data of the last sync ("As of …") and opens even when Odoo is down.
