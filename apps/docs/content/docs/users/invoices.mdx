Open **Invoices** in the sidebar. The list shows **Number**, **Account**, **Issued**, **Due**, **Total**, and **Status**. Click a column header to sort by it. Overdue invoices are highlighted. Click an invoice number to open it.

## Create an invoice

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **New Invoice**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the **Type** (**INVOICE**, **CREDIT NOTE**, or **PROFORMA**), the **Account**, the **Invoice Series**, and the **Currency**. Set the **Due Date**.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally fill in bank details: **Variable Symbol**, **Bank Name**, **IBAN**, and **SWIFT**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Line Items**, click **Add Line** for each item. Pick a **Product** or type a **Description**, then set **Quantity**, **Unit Price**, **Discount %**, and **Tax Rate**. The line **Total** is calculated for you.
</Step>
<Step>
Add **Public Notes** (printed on the PDF) and **Internal Notes** (for your team) if needed.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save Draft**.
</Step>
</Steps>

You need an account and at least one line item to save.

## Invoice lifecycle

A new invoice is a draft. Drafts have no number yet and show **DRAFT** in place of the number.

| Status | How it gets there |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | You saved a new invoice. |
| **Issued** | You clicked **Issue** on a draft. The invoice gets its number from the series and a PDF is generated. |
| **Sent** | You sent an issued invoice by email. |
| **Partially Paid** | Payments recorded so far are less than the total. |
| **Paid** | Payments cover the full total. |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled a draft. |

<Callout type="warn">
Once issued, an invoice can no longer be edited or cancelled. Check the draft before you click **Issue**. To correct an issued invoice, use **Duplicate** to start a new draft from it.
</Callout>

## Actions on an invoice

The buttons at the top of the invoice detail page depend on its status.

- **Edit**, **Issue**, **Cancel**: drafts only. Editing opens the same form, with **Update Draft** to save.
- **Send by Email**: issued or sent invoices. Enter the recipient in **To** (prefilled with the account's email), and optionally a **Subject (optional)** and **Message (optional)**. The PDF is attached.
- **Add Payment**: issued, sent, partially paid, or overdue invoices.
- **Duplicate**: copies the invoice into a new draft.
- **Download PDF**: opens the PDF.
- **Regenerate PDF**: issued invoices. Re-renders the PDF with the current company settings.

## Record a payment

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the invoice and click **Add Payment**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the **Amount** and **Date**. Choose a **Method**: Bank Transfer, Cash, Card, Check, PayPal, or Other.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally add a **Reference** (for example the transaction ID) and a **Note**. Click **Record Payment**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The status changes to **Partially Paid** or **Paid** depending on the remaining balance. Recorded payments appear in the **Payments** section.

## Invoice detail page

Besides the actions, the detail page shows:

- **Details**: account, address, issue and due dates, variable symbol, and series.
- **Financial Summary**: **Subtotal**, **Discount**, **VAT**, **Grand Total**, **Paid**, and **Balance Due**, plus bank details.
- **Line Items**, **Public Notes**, and **Internal Notes**.
- **Payments**: the payments recorded so far.
- **Activity**: a log of what happened to the invoice.

Invoices for an account also appear on that account's detail page. See [Accounts and contacts](/docs/users/crm/accounts-contacts).

Invoice series, tax rates, and company details on the PDF are set by an admin. See the [admin guide](/docs/admins).
