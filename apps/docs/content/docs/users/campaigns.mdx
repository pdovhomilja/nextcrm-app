title: Campaigns

Campaigns send marketing email to people on your target lists. Open **Campaigns** in the sidebar. It has four pages: **All Campaigns**, **Templates**, **Targets**, and **Target Lists**.

The usual order is: add targets, group them into a target list, write a template, then create a campaign for that list.

<Callout type="info">
With the user role you see only the campaigns, templates, targets, and target lists you created. Managers and admins see everyone's.
</Callout>

## Targets

A target is a person or company you want to reach. Targets are separate from CRM contacts until you convert them.

To add a target, open **Targets** and click **+ New Target**. Fill in the fields you know (name, email, phone, company, position, websites, social profiles, city, country, and so on) and save.

To import targets from a file:

<Steps>
<Step>
On **Targets**, click **Import CSV / Excel** and then **Select file**. Use a `.csv` or `.xlsx` file.
</Step>
<Step>
Map your file's columns to target fields. AI pre-fills suggestions; change any mapping or set it to skip. Click **Next**.
</Step>
<Step>
Check the preview and start the import. Each row needs at least a last name or a company. Rows without either are skipped and listed in the result.
</Step>
</Steps>

To export targets, click **Export** and choose **Export CSV** or **Export Excel**. If you selected rows, only those are exported. Otherwise the export contains the rows that match your current filter.

### Target detail

Click a target to open it. From there you can:

- **Enrich with AI**: start a background search that fills in missing details. See [Enriching targets](#enriching-targets).
- **Convert to deal**: create a company account, a contact, and an opportunity from the target, then open the new opportunity. Converting the same target again reuses the records already created.
- **Add Contact**: add a person at the target's company (name, email, phone, LinkedIn URL).

### Enriching targets

Enrichment looks up public information about a target on the web and fills in empty fields. It never overwrites data you already entered. A target needs an email or a company name before it can be enriched.

- One target: open it and click **Enrich with AI**. The job runs in the background.
- Several targets: select rows in the **Targets** table and click **Enrich N targets**. Pick the fields to fill and start. You can enrich up to 100 targets at once.

To follow progress, open `/campaigns/targets/enrichment` (the **Target Enrichment Jobs** page). It lists each job with its status, fields, start time, and who started it. Click **Retry** on a job that failed.

<Callout type="warn">
Enrichment needs an AI provider key. If no key is set for your organization, add your own under [Profile → LLMs](/docs/users/profile) or ask an admin.
</Callout>

## Target lists

A target list is the audience for a campaign.

1. Open **Target Lists** and click **+ New List**.
2. Enter a **Name** and an optional **Description**, then click **Create**.
3. Open the list and click **+ Add Target**. Search by name, email, or company, tick the targets, and click **Add Selected**.

To take a target off a list, click the trash icon next to it. You can also export a list's targets with **Export**.

## Templates

A template holds the subject line and body of an email so you can reuse it.

To create a template, open **Templates** and click **+ New Template**. Enter a **Template Name**, an optional **Description**, and a **Subject Line**, then write the body under **Email Body**. Switch to **Preview** to see the rendered email. Click **Save Template**.

To draft the body with AI, describe the email under **Generate with AI** and click **Generate**. This uses an OpenAI key; if none is available, add one under [Profile → LLMs](/docs/users/profile).

You can personalise subject and body with merge tags. NextCRM replaces them with each recipient's data:

| Tag | Replaced with |
| --- | --- |
| `{{first_name}}` | First name |
| `{{last_name}}` | Last name |
| `{{email}}` | Email |
| `{{company}}` | Company |
| `{{position}}` | Position |

## Create a campaign

Open **All Campaigns** and click **+ New Campaign**. The wizard has four steps.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Details.** Enter a **Campaign Name** (required), and optionally a **Description**, **From Name**, and **Reply-to Email**.
</Step>
<Step>
**Template.** Either **Generate with AI** from a short description, or **Choose Existing** to pick a saved template. Check the **Subject Line** and **Email Content**.
</Step>
<Step>
**Audience.** Select one or more target lists. The step shows how many targets each list holds.
</Step>
<Step>
**Schedule.** Under **When to send**, pick **Send now** or **Schedule for later** and set the date and time. Optionally click **+ Add follow-up** to add more emails (see below). Click **Submit Campaign**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
Only managers and admins can send or schedule a campaign. If you have the user role, ask a manager to send it for you.
</Callout>

### Follow-up steps

Each follow-up has:

- **Delay (days after previous)**: how long to wait after the previous email.
- **Send to**: **All recipients** or **Non-openers only**.
- **Template** and **Subject** for the follow-up email.

Follow-ups go only to recipients whose first email was sent and who have not unsubscribed.

### Unsubscribes

Every campaign email contains an unsubscribe link. A target who unsubscribes is never emailed again by any campaign. If you import the same email address later, it stays unsubscribed.

## Track a campaign

The **All Campaigns** table shows each campaign's status (draft, scheduled, sending, sent, paused), scheduled time, recipient count, and template. Filter by name or status.

Click a campaign to see:

- Totals for **Sent**, **Delivered**, **Open Rate**, **Click Rate**, and **Bounced**.
- **Campaign Steps**: each email in the sequence with its own sent and opened counts.
- **Recipients**: everyone the campaign was sent to. Search by name or email and filter by status.

To stop a scheduled or sending campaign, click **Pause** on its detail page.

To delete a campaign, open the row menu in **All Campaigns** and choose **Delete**.

For totals across all campaigns, see [Reports](/docs/users/reports).
