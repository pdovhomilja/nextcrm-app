---
title: Companies and contacts
description: Add companies (accounts) and the people who work there, and see everything linked to them.
---

In the sidebar, companies appear as **Companies**. On pages and forms they are called accounts. Both words mean the same record.

## Add a company

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Sales** → **Companies** and click **+**.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in **Account name** (required) and any other fields: **Office phone**, **E-mail**, **Website**, **Account ID**, **Account VAT number**, billing and shipping address, **Annual revenue**, **Is member of** (a parent company), and **Choose industry**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create account**.
</Step>
</Steps>

If your admin has installed a company registry plugin, a country selector and a **Load from registry** button appear under **Account ID**. Enter the registration number, pick the country, and click **Load from registry** to fill in the company details. If nothing is found, you see "Company not found in the registry."

## The company page

The **Overview** tab shows the company's details, billing and shipping address, and these cards:

- **Case study:** mark a happy client as a reference.
- **Activities:** calls, meetings, notes, and emails. See [Activities and tasks](/docs/users/crm/activities-tasks).
- **Find Similar:** see [Working with CRM records](/docs/users/crm/records#find-similar-records).
- **Tasks:** follow-up tasks for this company.
- **Opportunities**, **Contacts**, **Contracts**, **Leads:** records linked to the company, each with **+** to add a new one already linked.
- **Products:** products and services this company has. See [Contracts and products](/docs/users/crm/contracts-products#assign-a-product-to-a-company).
- **Documents** and **Invoices** linked to the company.

### Case studies

To suggest a company as a case study, click **Flag as candidate** on the **Case study** card. The card shows a **Candidate** badge. To take it back, click **Withdraw candidacy**.

Managers and admins see **Approve case study** on candidates. After approval the badge reads **Approved**, and they can click **Revoke approval** to undo it. Marketing should only reference approved companies.

## Add a contact

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Sales** → **Contacts** and click **+**. Or click **+** on the **Contacts** card of a company page to link the contact to that company.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in **Last name** (required) and any other fields, such as **First name**, **Position**, **Email**, **Personal email**, **Mobile phone**, **Office phone**, **Birthday - (optional)**, and social profiles.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose **Assign an Account** to link the contact to a company, **Assigned user** for the owner, and **Contact type**: **Customer**, **Partner**, or **Vendor**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create contact**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Always enter the contact's email address. NextCRM uses it to link synced emails and calendar meetings to the contact, and AI enrichment needs it.
</Callout>

## The contact page

The **Overview** tab shows the contact's details with an **Enrich with AI** button (see [AI enrichment](/docs/users/crm/ai-enrichment)), then **Activities**, **Find Similar**, and the contact's companies, opportunities, and documents.

## Emails and meetings on companies and contacts

When you connect a mailbox in your profile, NextCRM checks each synced message. If the From, To, or Cc address matches the email of a contact or a company, the message is linked to that record. Bcc addresses are ignored. See [Email](/docs/users/emails).

When you connect Google Calendar, meetings with a contact's email address become **Meeting** activities on the contact and the contact's company. See [Activities and tasks](/docs/users/crm/activities-tasks#meetings-from-google-calendar).
