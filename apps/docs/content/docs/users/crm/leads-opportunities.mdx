---
title: Leads, opportunities, and approvals
description: Track potential customers, move deals through the pipeline, and get quotes approved.
---

A **lead** is a person or company that might buy. An **opportunity** (a deal) is a specific sale you are working on, linked to a company.

## Add a lead

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Sales** → **Leads** and click **+**.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in **Last name** (required), and as needed **First name**, **Company**, **Job title**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Lead source**, **Referred by**, and **Campaign**.
</Step>
<Step>
Use **Assign an account** to link the lead to an existing company.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create lead**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Lead statuses are **New**, **In progress**, and **Completed**. The lead page shows the lead's details, **Activities**, **Find Similar**, and the **History** tab.

## Add an opportunity

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Sales** → **Opportunities** and click **+**. Or click **+** on the **Opportunities** card of a company page.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in **Opportunity name** (required), **Expected close date**, **Sales type**, **Sale stage**, **Budget**, **Currency**, **Expected revenue**, and **Next step**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the **Assigned Account** and, if needed, a contact and campaign.
</Step>
<Step>
Save the form.
</Step>
</Steps>

Opportunity statuses are **Active**, **Inactive**, **Pending**, and **Closed**.

## The opportunity page

The **Overview** tab shows the deal's details, then:

- **Line items:** the products and services in the quote. Click **+** to open **Add Line Item**. Pick a product under **Product (optional)** or type a custom **Name**, then set **Quantity**, **Unit Price**, and a **Discount Type** (**None**, **Percentage (%)**, or **Fixed Amount**). The table shows each **Line Total** and the **Subtotal**.
- **Activities** and **Find Similar**.
- The deal's companies, contacts, and documents.

## The pipeline board

**Sales** → **Dashboard** shows active opportunities as cards in columns, one column per sales stage.

- **Change the stage:** drag a card to another column. You see "Opportunity stage changed".
- **Add a deal in a stage:** click the plus icon in the column header.
- **Mark a deal as lost:** click the thumbs-down icon on the card. The deal becomes inactive and moves to the **Lost** column.

**Sales** → **My Dashboard** lists your open tasks, leads, and active opportunities in **My Tasks**, **My Leads**, and **My Opportunities**.

## Quote approval

Before a quote or statement of work goes to a client, a manager or admin can sign it off.

<Steps>
<Step>
On the opportunity page, click **Request approval**. All active managers and admins get an email.
</Step>
<Step>
The deal shows an **Approval pending** badge.
</Step>
<Step>
After the decision you get an email, and the badge changes to **Approved** or **Rejected**. Hover over **Rejected** to read the reviewer's note.
</Step>
</Steps>

After a rejection, fix the quote and click **Request approval** again.

<Callout type="warn">
If your admin has marked a sales stage as the quote stage, you cannot move a deal into that stage until its quote is approved. You see "Quote approval is required before moving this deal to the Qualified stage."
</Callout>

### Approve or reject (managers and admins)

Go to **Sales** → **Approvals**. The **Quote approvals** page lists deals waiting for a decision, with **Deal**, **Account**, **Rep**, **Budget**, and **Requested**.

- Click **Approve** to approve the quote.
- Click **Reject**, write what needs to change in the dialog, and click **Reject quote**. The note goes to the rep by email.

## Automatic follow-up

Your admin can link sales stages to automatic follow-up. When that is set up:

- **Follow-up tasks:** moving a deal into the quote stage, or into a care stage after delivery, creates follow-up tasks for the deal's owner on a schedule. Moving the deal to another stage cancels the remaining ones.
- **Closing silent deals:** a deal in the quote stage with no client activity for a set number of days (45 by default) is closed, and the owner gets an email. An activity logged on the deal, or a synced incoming email linked to the deal's contact or company, resets the clock.

Independent of stages, NextCRM checks once a week for contracts and assigned products whose renewal date is coming up (within 30 days by default) and creates a renewal task for them.

Automatic tasks are assigned to the owner of the deal, contract, or company, who also gets an email. They appear in the **Tasks** card of the linked company. Ask your admin which stages are linked.
