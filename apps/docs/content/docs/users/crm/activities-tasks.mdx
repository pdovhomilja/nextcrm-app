## Activities

Companies, contacts, leads, opportunities, and contracts each have an **Activities** card on the **Overview** tab. It lists the record's calls, meetings, notes, and emails, newest first. Click **Load more** to see older ones.

### Log an activity

<Steps>
<Step>
On the record page, click **Log activity**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a **Type**: **Call**, **Meeting**, **Note**, or **Email**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **Title \*** and a **Date & Time \***.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the **Status**: **Scheduled**, **Completed**, or **Cancelled**. Calls and meetings start as **Scheduled**, notes and emails as **Completed**.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in the extra fields for the type: **Duration (minutes)** and **Outcome** for calls and meetings, **Email Subject** for emails. Add **Notes** if needed.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Log activity**.
</Step>
</Steps>

To change an activity, click the pencil icon, edit the fields, and click **Save changes**. A common case is setting a finished call to **Completed** and filling in the **Outcome**.

To remove an activity, click the trash icon and confirm **Delete** in the **Delete activity?** dialog.

You can edit and delete activities you created. Managers and admins can edit and delete any activity.

## Meetings from Google Calendar

After you connect Google Calendar in your profile (see [Profile and settings](/docs/users/profile#calendar)), NextCRM reads your calendar every 15 minutes.

For each event, NextCRM looks at the attendees' email addresses:

1. If an attendee matches a contact, the event becomes a **Meeting** activity on that contact and on the contact's company. If the company has exactly one active opportunity, the meeting is linked to it as well.
2. Otherwise, if an attendee matches a campaign target, the meeting is linked to the target.
3. Otherwise, if an attendee matches a lead, the meeting is linked to the lead.

Events with no matching attendee are skipped. When an event is moved in Google Calendar, the activity date follows. When it is cancelled, the activity status changes to **Cancelled**.

### Two-way sync

With **Enable two-way sync** turned on in your profile, meetings also flow the other way:

- When you log a **Meeting** activity with a future date, NextCRM creates the event in your Google Calendar. The linked contact (or target, or lead) is added as an attendee and **receives an invitation from Google**.
- When you change the meeting's title, notes, date, or duration, the Google event is updated and attendees get an updated invitation.
- When you delete the meeting activity, the Google event is cancelled and attendees are notified.

<Callout type="warn">
Two-way sync sends real invitations to your customers. Check the date and the linked contact before you save a meeting.
</Callout>

NextCRM does not push meetings dated in the past, and it does not change events that someone else organized.

## Company tasks

Company tasks are follow-ups attached to a company, separate from project tasks. They appear on the **Tasks** card of the company page.

To create one, click **+** on the **Tasks** card. In **Create new Task**, fill in **New task name**, **Task description**, **Task due date**, **Choose task priority**, and **Assigned to**, then click **Create**.

In the card's row menu, **View** opens the task and **Delete** removes it. The task page shows **Task details**, attached documents, and a **Team conversation**. To comment, type in **Your comment ...** and click **Add**.

Your own open company tasks also appear on **Sales** → **My Dashboard** under **My Tasks**. For project boards and project tasks, see [Projects](/docs/users/projects).
