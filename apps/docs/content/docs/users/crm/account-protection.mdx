title: Account protection
description: Keep your companies protected, see who owned them, and handle companies that are already in the CRM.

Account protection gives each company one owner. When you become the owner of a company, it is protected for a set time. To keep it, you log a visit or meeting with the company in time.

<Callout type="info">
  This page applies only when your admin has installed the **Account protection** plugin. The number of days and the activity types that count are set by your admin. The examples use the defaults: 90 days of protection and 30 days to log a visit or meeting. For the admin side, see [Account protection](/docs/admins/plugins/account-protection).
</Callout>

## How protection works

- A company is identified by its **Account ID** (registration number) and **Billing country**. A company without an **Account ID** is not protected.
- When you create a company with yourself in **Assigned to**, or a manager assigns it to you, protection starts.
- You must log a qualifying contact within the contact period (default 30 days). If you don't, you lose the company.
- With a contact logged, the company stays yours until the end of the protection period (default 90 days). Then you lose it, and a manager can assign it again.

Losing a company means its owner is cleared. This happens in a daily run in the morning.

## Keep a company protected

Log a completed visit or meeting on the company.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the company and click **Log activity**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose **Visit** or **Meeting** as the **Type**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **Title \***, the **Date & Time \*** of the visit, and set **Status** to **Completed**. Visits and meetings start as **Scheduled**, which does not count.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Log activity**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The activity must be linked to the company and dated on or after the day protection started. An activity logged only on a contact or an opportunity does not count unless it is also linked to the company.

A contact does not extend protection past the end date. Only a manager can start a new protection period by assigning the company again.

See [Activities and tasks](/docs/users/crm/activities-tasks#log-an-activity).

## Check a company's protection

The company page shows one line with the status:

- "Protected until \{date\}": the company is protected, and a contact is logged (or not needed).
- "Contact needed by \{date\}": log a visit or meeting by this date.
- "Not protected: no owner": nobody owns the company.
- "Not protected: no registration number": the company has no **Account ID**.

The **Protection** tab shows more:

- **Status:** the same line as above.
- **Qualifying contact:** "Yes, on \{date\}" or "Not yet".
- **Owner history:** every owner change with the date, the previous and new owner, who made the change, and the reason. "system" means the change was made by a background job.

## When a company is already in the CRM

You cannot create a second company with the same **Account ID** and country. This also applies when you change the **Account ID** or **Billing country** of an existing company. You see one of these messages:

- "You already have this company in the CRM." You own the existing company. Search for it in **Companies**.
- "This company is protected until \{until\}." Another user owns it. Your manager is told about the attempt.
- "This company is already in the CRM. A manager has been asked to assign it to you." Nobody owns it. Your manager gets an email and can assign it to you.

The messages never show who owns the company.

If your admin requires it, you see "Enter the company registration number." when you create a company without an **Account ID**.

## Change a company's owner

Only managers and admins can change **Assigned to** on an existing company. If you try, you see "Only a manager can change the account owner." Ask your manager to reassign it.

## For managers

### Expiring protection

Go to **Plugins → Expiring protection** in the sidebar. The page shows:

- **Due within \{days\} days:** companies whose contact deadline or protection end is coming up. Use it to remind reps to log their visits.
- **Freed in the last 7 days:** companies that lost their owner. Assign them to a rep if needed.

A company can stay in the first list until the next morning run, even when its contact is already logged.

### Emails you get

As a manager or admin, you get emails when:

- a rep tries to create a company that someone else protects ("Blocked: \{account\} is protected");
- a rep tries to create a company that is in the CRM without an owner ("Assign \{account\}");
- the daily run removes owners ("\{count\} accounts lost their protection"), with the list of companies.

To assign a company, open it, edit it, and set **Assigned to**. The new owner gets a new protection period, and the change is added to the **Owner history**.

You also cannot create a second company with the same number. You see "This company is already in the CRM." or "This company is protected until \{until\}." and can open the existing company instead.
