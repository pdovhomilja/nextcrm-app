---
title: User guide
description: How to use NextCRM day to day, for sales people and managers.
---

This guide is for people who sign in to NextCRM and work with customers: sales reps, account managers, and their managers. It covers the web interface only. Installing and configuring NextCRM is in the [admin guide](/docs/admins), and the API and MCP server are in the [developer guide](/docs/developers).

Some features depend on your role (user, manager, or admin) and on what your administrator has set up, such as Google sign-in, Google Calendar, or AI keys. Each page says when that applies.
