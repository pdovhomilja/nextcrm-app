title: Projects and tasks

Open **Projects** in the sidebar. The page lists the projects you can see and has buttons for **New project**, **New task**, **All tasks**, **My tasks**, and **Dashboard**.

You see a project if you created it, it is shared with you, you watch it, or its visibility is **Public**. Managers and admins see all projects.

## Create a project

<Steps>
<Step>
On the **Projects** page, click **New project**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **Project name** and a **Description**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a **Visibility**: **Public** or **Private**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create**.
</Step>
</Steps>

To open a project, click its name in the list, or use the row menu and choose **View detail**. The same menu has **Edit**, **Watch project**, **Stop watching**, and **Delete**.

## Work on the board

Each project is a kanban board made of sections (columns). Tasks sit inside sections as cards.

- **Add a section:** click **New section** at the top of the board, or the plus icon after the last column. Enter a **Section name** and click **Create**.
- **Rename a section:** type into the section title field. The new title saves automatically.
- **Delete a section:** click the trash icon next to the section's task count.
- **Move a task:** drag the card to another position or another section.
- **Add a task quickly:** click the plus icon at the top of a section. This creates an untitled task in that section, which you can then edit.

A warning icon on a card means the task is overdue. A check mark means it is done.

Each card has a menu with **View**, **Edit**, **Mark as done**, and **Delete**. **Edit** and **Mark as done** are hidden once a task is complete.

To delete the whole project, click the red trash button at the top right of the board and confirm in the **Delete project** dialog.

## Create a task

You can create a task from two places:

<Tabs items={["From the Projects page", "From a board"]}>
<Tab value="From the Projects page">
Click **New task**. Fill in **Task name**, **Description**, **Due date**, **Assigned to**, **Project**, and **Priority**, then click **Create**. The task goes into the chosen project.
</Tab>
<Tab value="From a board">
Click **Create new task**. Fill in **New task name**, **Task description**, **Due date**, **Assigned to**, **Choose section**, and **Choose task priority**, then click **Create**.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

Priority can be **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, or **Critical**.

<Callout type="warn">
Deleting a task removes it permanently, together with its comments.
</Callout>

## Task detail

Open a task with **View** to see its due date, priority, status, and the people involved. Under **Task Actions:** you can click **Mark as done** or **Edit**.

The detail page also shows:

- **Task documents**: files attached to this task. Use the row menu to **View** a file or **Disconnect from task**.
- **Available documents**: other documents in the system. Use **Connect to task** to attach one.
- **Team conversation**: comments on the task. Type into **Your comment ...** and click **Add**.

Documents are uploaded on the Documents page. See [Documents](/docs/users/documents).

## Task lists

- **All tasks** lists tasks in a table you can filter by name (**Filter tasks...**), **Status**, and **Priority**.
- **My tasks** lists the tasks assigned to you.

Task status is **Active**, **Pending**, or **Complete**.

## Projects dashboard

Click **Dashboard** on the Projects page, or **Todo Dashboard** in your user menu at the bottom of the sidebar. The dashboard shows two groups of open tasks:

- **Tasks due Today**: tasks whose due date has arrived or passed.
- **Tasks due in 7 days**: tasks due within the next week.

Each card links to the task (**View task**) and lets you open the **Chat** (the task's team conversation), **Mark as done**, or **Edit** it. With the user role you see your own tasks here. Managers and admins see everyone's.
